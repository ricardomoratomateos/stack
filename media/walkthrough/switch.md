## Jumping between tasks

`Cmd+Shift+]` and `Cmd+Shift+[` (Ctrl on Linux) move between worktrees. Clicking a card does the same.

Switching is instant: text search, Quick Open and breakpoints are scoped to the worktree you are in, so five parallel tasks never drown each other out. Turn on **focus mode** in settings if you also want the other worktrees' tabs and terminals out of the way.
